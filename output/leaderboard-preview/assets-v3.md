## Medal prompt
Reference image is exact target style. Extract/reconstruct ONLY the three rank badges shown at left of rows 1 2 3: gold 1, silver 2, bronze 3. Preserve their EXACT simple shape and style: five tipped crown forming the top of a long narrow angular shield, extruded beveled border, large number, NO laurel leaves, NO extra ornaments, no black leather, no jewels. Sprite sheet, three equal columns, every badge centered with same height and size. Pure solid black background #000000, no glow spilling outside badges, no text other than 1 2 3. Front view same as reference. Tight margins, badges fill height. This is a UI asset extraction, not a redesign.

## Background prompt
Remove the entire UI overlay from this reference image and reconstruct ONLY its exact background wallpaper. Preserve the poker chip foreground bottom 15 percent, with large black gold Poker21 chip bottom center, black gold chips bottom left, same camera perspective, same lighting and bokeh. Preserve dark black/gold abstract background at sides and top. Replace all cards, all text, avatars, medals, buttons, search with an almost black continuous subtle background. Exact original portrait aspect ratio. Do not redesign or move foreground poker chips. This output is the wallpaper underneath the original UI.

## Dragon prompt
Extract the golden dragon ornament visible behind the first player's card in this reference. Output only this dark antique-gold Asian dragon on a PURE BLACK #000000 background, centered square. Elegant long serpentine body curled downwards, fierce head turned left at top, fine embossed scales and whiskers, same subtle gold-on-black look as the reference. No card, no interface, no lettering, no people. This is a low-contrast decorative watermark asset, not a bright hero illustration.
