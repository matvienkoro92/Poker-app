# Cue collection v2

Eight separate assets generated with the built-in image editor, saved to `assets/player-hall/billiards-cue-{id}-v2.webp`. Transparent outputs are trimmed to alpha bounds and encoded losslessly. The same assets are used in the cue collection and the leaning cue preview. Game cue materials are generated as cylindrical inlay textures, with matching color, grip and metal definitions in `app-billiards-cues.js`.

Shared prompt: Single full-length professional billiards cue isolated on truly transparent background. Tall vertical portrait, perfectly straight cue centered, blue chalked leather tip at top and rubber butt bumper at bottom. Realistic slim taper, whole cue including shaft, fine materials, warm side reflections, no floor, no pedestal, no cast shadow, no text. No bent shaft or multiple cues.
